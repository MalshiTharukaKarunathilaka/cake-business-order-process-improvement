# Business Requirements

## Overview

The business requirements define what the improved cake order management process needs to achieve from a business perspective.

These requirements are based on the current order process, identified pain points, root cause analysis, and stakeholder needs.

## Business Requirements

### BR-01: Reliable Order Tracking

The business should have a reliable method for recording and tracking all confirmed customer orders from confirmation until completion.

**Reason:**

The current process relies on manually recording orders in a calendar and pinning WhatsApp conversations, which creates a risk of orders being overlooked.

---

### BR-02: Centralized Order Information

The business should have a single place where important information related to a customer order can be accessed.

The information should include, where applicable:

- Customer details
- Cake design and flavour
- Required date
- Required time
- Cake size
- Pickup or delivery option
- Delivery address
- Payment status
- Order status

**Reason:**

Currently, order information is distributed between WhatsApp conversations, pinned chats, and the calendar.

---

### BR-03: Reduce Manual Tracking

The improved process should reduce the owner's dependence on memory and repetitive manual tracking activities.

**Reason:**

The current process requires the owner to remember to update multiple tracking methods after an order is confirmed.

---

### BR-04: Improve Upcoming Order Visibility

The business should be able to easily identify upcoming confirmed orders and their required dates and times.

**Reason:**

The owner needs to identify upcoming orders early enough to plan cake preparation.

---

### BR-05: Support Preparation Planning

The order management process should provide enough information for the owner to determine when preparation needs to begin based on the required date and time.

For example, morning orders may need to be prepared on the previous day, while later orders may be prepared early on the required date.

**Reason:**

The current preparation process depends on the owner identifying upcoming orders in time.

---

### BR-06: Track Payment Status

The process should allow the business to identify whether the required payments for an order have been received.

The process should distinguish between:

- Deposit pending
- 50% deposit received
- Remaining payment pending
- Full payment completed

**Reason:**

Customers pay 50% to confirm an order and must complete the remaining payment before the cake is handed over.

---

### BR-07: Support Pickup and Delivery Orders

The order management process should distinguish between customer pickup and delivery orders.

For delivery orders, the relevant delivery information should be available to the owner.

**Reason:**

The business handles both pickup and delivery orders.

---

### BR-08: Improve Order Retrieval

The business should be able to quickly retrieve an existing order without relying on searching through old WhatsApp conversations.

**Reason:**

During the observed incident, the owner had to search through the customer's WhatsApp conversation to confirm the order.

---

### BR-09: Reduce the Risk of Missed Orders

The improved process should reduce the likelihood of a confirmed order being overlooked before its required date.

**Reason:**

The observed incident demonstrated that an order can become difficult to identify when manual tracking actions are missed.

---

### BR-10: Support Order Completion

The process should allow the business to track an order from confirmation through preparation, payment completion, and final pickup or delivery.

**Reason:**

An order involves several stages that need to be completed before the customer receives the cake.

---

## Expected Business Outcomes

The improved order management process should:

- Reduce the risk of missed or forgotten orders.
- Give the owner better visibility of upcoming orders.
- Reduce the need to search through old WhatsApp conversations.
- Make important order information easier to access.
- Improve preparation planning.
- Make payment status easier to track.
- Improve the reliability of pickup and delivery preparation.
- Provide customers with a more reliable ordering experience.
