# Stakeholder Analysis

## Overview

The current cake order process involves several stakeholders who either provide information, perform activities, support the order process, or are affected by the successful completion of an order.

Understanding the stakeholders and their needs helps identify what an improved order management process should support.

## Stakeholders

### 1. Business Owner

**Role:** Primary person responsible for managing the cake order process.

The business owner communicates with customers, collects order requirements, confirms payments, records upcoming orders, prepares cakes, and coordinates pickup or delivery.

**Main Responsibilities:**

- Receive customer orders.
- Collect cake requirements.
- Confirm the order and 50% deposit.
- Record upcoming orders.
- Monitor upcoming orders.
- Prepare cakes according to the required date and time.
- Confirm the remaining payment.
- Arrange pickup or delivery.
- Coordinate with third-party delivery services when required.

**Key Needs:**

- A reliable way to track all confirmed orders.
- Easy access to complete order information.
- Visibility of upcoming orders.
- Clear indication of required dates and times.
- Reduced dependence on memory and manual tracking.
- Ability to identify orders that require preparation.
- Easy access to delivery information when arranging a delivery.

**Main Concern:**

Missing an order or discovering it too late can affect preparation time and customer satisfaction.

---

### 2. Customer

**Role:** Person who places and receives the cake order.

The customer provides the cake requirements and delivery or pickup information and makes the required payments.

**Main Responsibilities:**

- Provide cake requirements.
- Provide the required date.
- Provide contact details.
- Select pickup or delivery.
- Provide the delivery address when required.
- Pay the 50% deposit.
- Pay the remaining balance before handover.
- Collect the cake or receive the delivery.

**Key Needs:**

- Confirmation that the order has been recorded.
- Confidence that the cake will be ready on the agreed date.
- Accurate recording of cake requirements.
- Clear communication regarding payment and delivery or pickup.
- Reliable completion of the order.

**Main Concern:**

The customer expects the business to remember and fulfill the confirmed order as agreed.

---

### 3. Third-Party Delivery Service

**Role:** Provides delivery services when the customer selects delivery instead of pickup.

The business owner uses third-party services such as PickMe or Uber to deliver completed cake orders to customers.

**Main Responsibilities:**

- Accept the delivery request.
- Collect the cake from the specified location.
- Deliver the cake to the customer's provided address.
- Provide delivery status information where available.

**Key Needs:**

- Accurate delivery address.
- Correct customer contact number.
- Correct pickup and delivery timing.
- Cake to be ready when the driver arrives.

**Main Concern:**

Incorrect delivery information, late preparation, or delays in arranging the delivery can affect the customer's ability to receive the cake on time.

---

## Stakeholder Summary

| Stakeholder | Role | Main Need | Potential Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business Owner | Manages the complete order process | Reliable order tracking | High |
| Customer | Places and receives the order | Order completed as agreed | High |
| Third-Party Delivery Service | Delivers cakes when delivery is requested | Accurate delivery information and timing | Medium |

## Stakeholder Interest and Influence

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business Owner | High | High | Directly manages the order process |
| Customer | High | Medium | Depends on the business to fulfill the order |
| Third-Party Delivery Service | Medium | Low | Supports delivery but does not manage the customer order |

## Stakeholder Needs Related to Process Improvement

### Business Owner

The improved process should reduce the amount of manual tracking required from the owner and make upcoming orders easier to identify and manage.

The owner should be able to access important order information without having to search through multiple WhatsApp conversations.

### Customer

The improved process should provide greater confidence that confirmed orders are properly recorded and will be completed according to the agreed requirements.

### Third-Party Delivery Service

The improved process should ensure that the required delivery information is available when a delivery is arranged, including the customer's contact number, delivery address, and required timing.

## Key Stakeholder Finding

The business owner is the primary stakeholder in the current process because most order management activities depend on the owner.

The customer is also a high-interest stakeholder because the reliability of the order management process directly affects the customer's experience.

Third-party delivery services such as PickMe and Uber act as external dependencies for delivery orders. They do not manage the cake order itself, but they are involved in the final delivery stage when delivery is selected.

An improved process should therefore primarily reduce the manual tracking burden on the business owner while improving order visibility and reliability for the customer and ensuring that accurate delivery information is available when required.
