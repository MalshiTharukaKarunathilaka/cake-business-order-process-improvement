# Problem Statement

## Problem

The cake business currently relies on WhatsApp conversations, pinned chats, and a personal calendar to manage customer orders. This process requires the business owner to manually transfer important order information from WhatsApp into the calendar and remember to pin relevant conversations.

When these manual steps are missed, an order can remain buried within the WhatsApp conversation history without being visible in the owner's normal order-tracking process.

## Observed Incident

A customer placed a cake order approximately one month before the required date. The owner did not add the order to the calendar or pin the customer's WhatsApp conversation.

The issue was discovered only one day before the required date when the customer asked whether a cake shown in a post was theirs. The owner was initially unsure and had to search through the previous WhatsApp messages. The search revealed that the customer had an order scheduled for the following day.

## Business Impact

This type of process failure can create several risks for the business:

- Orders may be overlooked or discovered too late.
- The owner may have insufficient preparation time.
- Customers may lose confidence in the business's ability to manage their orders.
- The business may experience reputational damage.
- The owner may spend additional time searching through old conversations.
- Similar issues may become more difficult to manage as the number of orders increases.

## Root Problem

The underlying problem is not simply that an order was forgotten. The current process relies heavily on manual recording and the owner's memory to transfer order information from WhatsApp into separate tracking methods.

There is no single, structured place where all upcoming orders can be reliably recorded, monitored, and updated.

## Business Analysis Focus

This case study will analyze the existing order management process, identify its pain points and root causes, and propose an improved process that provides a more reliable way to record, track, and manage customer orders.
