# Current Order Process

## Overview

The cake business currently manages customer orders mainly through WhatsApp, the owner's calendar, and pinned WhatsApp conversations.

Customers provide their cake requirements through WhatsApp, while the owner uses the calendar and pinned conversations to keep track of confirmed and upcoming orders. The process involves collecting the order details, confirming the order through a 50% payment, recording the order, preparing the cake, receiving the remaining payment, and completing the pickup or delivery.

## Current Process

### 1. Customer Places an Order

The customer contacts the business through WhatsApp to place a cake order.

The customer provides the required cake details, including:

* Cake design
* Cake flavor
* Required date
* Cake size
* Contact number

Any other specific requirements are discussed through the WhatsApp conversation.

### 2. Order and Delivery Details Are Confirmed

The owner discusses the order details with the customer and confirms the requirements.

The owner also determines whether the customer will:

* Pick up the cake, or
* Request delivery

If delivery is required, the customer provides the delivery address.

### 3. 50% Payment Is Made

The owner requests a 50% payment to confirm the order.

Once the required 50% payment is received, the order is considered confirmed.

### 4. Order Is Recorded

After confirming the order, the owner records the order in her calendar.

The relevant WhatsApp conversation is also pinned so that the customer conversation can be accessed more easily when needed.

The calendar and pinned WhatsApp chats act as the main methods for tracking upcoming orders.

### 5. Upcoming Orders Are Checked

The owner checks the calendar and pinned WhatsApp conversations to identify upcoming orders.

These records help the owner determine which cakes need to be prepared and when preparation needs to begin.

### 6. Cake Preparation

The owner plans the preparation according to the required pickup or delivery time.

For orders required in the morning, the cake may be prepared on the previous day.

For orders required later during the day, the owner starts preparing the cake early in the morning on the required date.

The preparation process therefore depends on the owner identifying the upcoming order and its required time.

### 7. Remaining 50% Payment Is Made

Before the cake is handed over to the customer, the remaining 50% payment must be completed.

The owner confirms that the full payment has been received before completing the order handover.

### 8. Pickup or Delivery

After the full payment has been received, the cake is either:

* Collected by the customer, or
* Delivered to the customer's provided address.

The order is then considered completed.

## Current Tracking Methods

| Method               | Purpose                                                          | Dependency                                                        |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| WhatsApp             | Customer communication and storing order details                 | The relevant conversation must be found                           |
| Pinned WhatsApp Chat | Provides quicker access to important customer conversations      | The owner must remember to pin the conversation                   |
| Calendar             | Records upcoming order dates and helps with preparation planning | The owner must remember to create and maintain the calendar entry |

## Key Process Characteristics

### Manual Recording

Order information is initially communicated through WhatsApp and then manually recorded in the owner's calendar.

### Multiple Tracking Methods

The business uses WhatsApp, pinned chats, and a calendar to manage information related to the same order.

### Reliance on Memory

The process depends on the owner remembering to perform important actions, particularly adding the order to the calendar and pinning the relevant WhatsApp conversation.

### Time-Dependent Preparation

Cake preparation depends on the required pickup or delivery time. Morning orders may require preparation on the previous day, while later orders can be prepared on the morning of the required date.

### Payment-Based Handover

The customer initially pays 50% to confirm the order and must pay the remaining 50% before the cake is handed over.

## Key Observation

The current process relies on several manual activities after an order is confirmed. Important order information remains within the WhatsApp conversation, while the date is separately recorded in the calendar and the conversation is separately pinned.

Because these activities depend on the owner remembering to complete them, there is a risk that an order may not be properly recorded or easily accessible when it is needed.

The previously observed missed-order incident demonstrates how this weakness can affect the business. In that situation, the customer had placed the order approximately one month in advance, but the order was not added to the calendar and the conversation was not pinned. The order was only discovered when the owner searched the WhatsApp conversation after the customer asked about the cake one day before the required date.
