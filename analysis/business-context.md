# Business Context

## Background

This case study focuses on a small home-based cake business that accepts customized cake orders through WhatsApp. Customers usually place their orders in advance and provide details such as the required date, cake design, and other customization requirements through the chat.

The business owner currently manages upcoming orders using a combination of WhatsApp conversations, pinned WhatsApp chats, and a personal calendar. After receiving an order, the owner needs to remember to record the relevant date in the calendar and pin the conversation so that the order can be easily found later.

As the number of orders increases, this process becomes dependent on manual actions and the owner's ability to remember to update each tracking method correctly.

## Observed Situation

An order was placed by a customer approximately one month before the required date. However, the order was not added to the owner's calendar and the WhatsApp conversation was not pinned.

The issue was discovered only when the customer later saw a post related to their cake and asked the owner whether the cake shown was theirs. The owner was initially unsure about the order and had to search through the WhatsApp conversation history. After checking the previous messages, the owner realized that the customer had an order scheduled for the following day.

This situation highlights a potential weakness in the current order management process, where important order information can remain inside individual WhatsApp conversations without being reliably transferred to a centralized tracking method.

## Business Context

The incident provides an opportunity to analyze how customer orders are currently received, recorded, tracked, and prepared. The purpose of this case study is to identify weaknesses in the existing process and propose a more reliable order management process that reduces the likelihood of missed orders while remaining practical for a small cake business.
