# Future State Process (TO-BE)

## Overview

The future-state process is designed to improve the way customer cake orders are recorded, tracked, prepared, and completed.

The main change is to move away from relying on WhatsApp conversations, pinned chats, and a separate calendar as the main methods of remembering orders.

Instead, confirmed orders should be maintained in a structured order tracking process where important order information can be accessed from one place.

The improved process is designed around the existing way the business operates and does not assume that the business needs to completely replace WhatsApp or third-party delivery services.

---

## Future Process Objectives

The improved process should:

1. Reduce the risk of forgetting or missing confirmed orders.
2. Make order information easier to find.
3. Reduce dependence on manually remembering to update multiple tracking methods.
4. Improve visibility of upcoming orders.
5. Support better cake preparation planning.
6. Make payment status easier to track.
7. Clearly distinguish pickup and delivery orders.
8. Make delivery information available when required.
9. Provide a clear view of the order status from confirmation to completion.

---

# Future Order Process

## Step 1: Customer Places an Order

The customer contacts the cake business and provides the required order information.

The information includes:

- Customer name
- Contact number
- Cake design
- Cake flavour
- Cake size
- Other customization requirements
- Required date
- Required time

The customer also indicates whether the order will be collected or delivered.

---

## Step 2: Owner Records the Order

The owner records the customer's order information in the order tracking process.

If the customer has selected delivery, the owner also records:

- Delivery address
- Customer contact number
- Required delivery time

The order remains pending until the required deposit is received.

---

## Step 3: Customer Pays the 50% Deposit

The customer makes the required 50% deposit to confirm the order.

The owner records the payment status.

### If the deposit has not been received:

The order remains:

**Pending Confirmation**

### If the deposit has been received:

The order becomes:

**Confirmed**

Only confirmed orders should be included in the owner's confirmed upcoming order list.

---

## Step 4: Order Becomes Visible as an Upcoming Order

Once the order is confirmed, it becomes part of the upcoming order list.

The owner can view:

- Customer
- Cake details
- Required date
- Required time
- Pickup or delivery
- Payment status
- Preparation status
- Order status

This reduces the need to remember which WhatsApp conversations were pinned or whether an order was manually added to the calendar.

---

## Step 5: Monitor Upcoming Orders

The owner regularly checks upcoming orders.

Orders approaching their required date are identified for preparation.

The owner can use the required date and time to decide when preparation should begin.

For example:

### Morning order

The cake may need to be prepared on the previous day.

### Later order

The owner may be able to begin preparation early on the required date.

---

## Step 6: Prepare the Cake

The owner prepares the cake according to the recorded order requirements.

The preparation status can be updated:

**Not Started → In Progress → Completed**

The recorded cake requirements can be referred to without searching through the original WhatsApp conversation.

---

## Step 7: Arrange Pickup or Delivery

The owner checks the order type.

### If Pickup

The customer collects the completed cake from the business.

### If Delivery

The owner arranges a third-party delivery service such as PickMe or Uber.

The owner has access to:

- Customer contact number
- Delivery address
- Required delivery time
- Order details

This information can be used when arranging the delivery.

---

## Step 8: Receive Remaining Payment

Before the cake is handed over to the customer, the remaining payment must be completed.

The owner checks the payment status.

### If remaining payment is pending:

The order should not be treated as fully completed.

### If remaining payment is received:

The payment status becomes:

**Full Payment Completed**

---

## Step 9: Complete the Order

After the cake has been successfully handed over to the customer or delivered, the owner marks the order as:

**Completed**

The completed order is no longer treated as an upcoming order.

---
