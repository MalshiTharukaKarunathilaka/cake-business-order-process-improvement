# Process Improvement Matrix

## Purpose

This document compares the current-state (AS-IS) process with the proposed future-state (TO-BE) process.

The purpose is to show how each identified weakness in the current process is addressed through the proposed improvements.

## AS-IS vs TO-BE

| Area | Current State (AS-IS) | Problem | Future State (TO-BE) | Expected Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Order recording | Customer order details are received through WhatsApp | Important information remains inside conversations | Order details are recorded in a structured order record | Easier access to order information |
| Order tracking | Owner relies on WhatsApp, pinned chats, and calendar | Tracking depends on multiple manual actions | Confirmed orders are maintained in an upcoming order list | Reduced reliance on memory |
| Calendar updates | Owner manually adds the order date to a calendar | Order can be missed if the calendar is not updated | Required date and time are part of the order record | Better visibility of upcoming orders |
| WhatsApp pinning | Owner manually pins important customer chats | Chat may not be pinned after confirmation | Order can be retrieved independently of pinned chats | Easier order retrieval |
| Order retrieval | Owner may need to search through WhatsApp history | Searching can take time and create uncertainty | Owner can search for the order directly | Faster access to previous orders |
| Cake requirements | Design, flavour, size, and other details are discussed through WhatsApp | Information may be difficult to find later | Cake requirements are recorded with the order | Reduced information retrieval effort |
| Payment tracking | Deposit and remaining payment are handled during the customer interaction | Payment status may not be immediately visible | Payment status is explicitly recorded | Better payment visibility |
| Upcoming orders | Owner checks previous dates and remembers upcoming orders | Orders may be discovered too late | Upcoming confirmed orders are clearly displayed | Better preparation planning |
| Preparation planning | Owner decides when to prepare based on manually checking orders | Missed orders can reduce preparation time | Required date and time are visible when planning preparation | More reliable preparation planning |
| Pickup / delivery | Pickup or delivery is discussed with the customer | Information may be mixed with chat history | Order type is recorded with the order | Clearer handover planning |
| Delivery information | Address and contact details are obtained through customer communication | Information may require searching | Delivery details are stored with the order | Easier delivery arrangement |
| Third-party delivery | PickMe or Uber is arranged separately | Delivery status may not be connected to order tracking | Delivery arrangement is recorded against the order | Better visibility of delivery stage |
| Order completion | Completion depends on the owner knowing the order has been handed over | Completed and upcoming orders can be harder to distinguish | Order can be marked as completed | Clearer order lifecycle |

## Key Improvements

### 1. From Multiple Tracking Methods to Structured Order Tracking

The current process depends on WhatsApp, pinned conversations, and a calendar.

The future process introduces a structured order record that contains the important information required to manage an order.

### 2. From Memory-Based Tracking to Visible Upcoming Orders

The owner should not have to rely mainly on remembering which orders are coming up.

Confirmed orders should be visible according to their required dates.

### 3. From Conversation-Based Retrieval to Order-Based Retrieval

WhatsApp can remain the main communication channel with customers, but the order information should not depend entirely on the WhatsApp conversation.

The owner should be able to retrieve the order information directly.

### 4. From Separate Payment Tracking to Explicit Payment Status

The future process makes the deposit and remaining payment status visible as part of the order.

### 5. From Manual Preparation Checking to Preparation Planning

The required date and time can be used to identify which orders need preparation and when preparation should begin.

## Link to Identified Pain Points

| Pain Point | Future Process Response |
|---|---|
| Order can be forgotten | Confirmed orders are maintained in a structured list |
| Owner depends on memory | Upcoming orders provide visibility |
| Information is spread across multiple places | Important information is recorded with the order |
| Searching WhatsApp takes time | Orders can be retrieved directly |
| Missed calendar updates | Required date is stored with the order |
| Missed WhatsApp pinning | Order tracking does not depend on pinned chats |
| Preparation can start too late | Upcoming orders show required dates and times |
| Payment status may be unclear | Payment status is explicitly recorded |

## Expected Business Impact

The proposed changes are expected to:

- Reduce the likelihood of missed orders.
- Improve visibility of upcoming work.
- Reduce the time spent searching for order information.
- Improve preparation planning.
- Make payment status easier to verify.
- Improve pickup and delivery coordination.
- Reduce dependence on memory and manual reminders.
