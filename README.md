# Cake Business Order Process Improvement

## Overview

This project is a Business Analysis case study focused on improving the order management process of a small home-based cake business.

The business currently receives customer orders mainly through WhatsApp and uses a combination of WhatsApp pinned chats and a personal calendar to keep track of upcoming orders.

The case study was inspired by an observed situation where a customer order was nearly missed because the order had not been added to the calendar and the WhatsApp conversation had not been pinned.

The purpose of this project is to analyze the existing order management process, identify its weaknesses, and propose a more reliable and practical process for managing customer orders.

## Business Problem

The current process depends on manually transferring order information from WhatsApp into other tracking methods such as a calendar and pinned chats.

When these steps are missed, important order information can remain buried within the WhatsApp conversation history. This can make it difficult for the business owner to identify upcoming orders and prepare them on time.

## Case Study

A customer placed a cake order approximately one month before the required date. However, the order was not added to the owner's calendar and the WhatsApp conversation was not pinned.

The issue was discovered only one day before the required date when the customer asked whether a cake shown in a post was theirs. The owner initially could not remember the order and had to search through the previous WhatsApp conversation. The order was then discovered to be scheduled for the following day.

This incident is used as the starting point for analyzing the current process and identifying opportunities for improvement.

## Objectives

This case study aims to:

* Understand the current customer order management process.
* Identify process gaps and potential failure points.
* Analyze the root causes of missed or difficult-to-track orders.
* Define business and functional requirements for an improved process.
* Design a more reliable future-state order management process.
* Demonstrate how Business Analysis techniques can be applied to a real-world small business scenario.
